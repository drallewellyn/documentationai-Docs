# Psychiatry Postgraduate Education Redesign — Evidence Base

A reference pack to inform the redesign of the **RANZCP** (Royal Australian and New
Zealand College of Psychiatrists) postgraduate psychiatry training program around
competency-based medical education (CBME).

## Contents

| Document | Purpose |
|---|---|

References newly folded in from the NFPT (Dean's) literature review are marked with ★ for review. |

## How to use this pack

- The **briefing paper** is the argument: read it first. It is written to be defensible
  in front of a committee that includes both CBME enthusiasts and sceptics.
- The **annotated bibliography** is the evidence: every claim in the briefing traces to a
  verified source here.

## Status of citations

All references were verified via a structured deep-research pass (multi-source,
adversarial verification). Where a remembered citation was wrong or out of date, the
**corrected** version is used and the change is noted in the bibliography.

> Scope note: this pack is an evidence/literature synthesis to support program design.
> It is not itself a curriculum or an accreditation submission.
